Who are we

Protecting the places that sustain us.

Soev Eye Africa is an environmental conservation and natural resource management organization dedicated to protecting, monitoring, and preserving the forests and natural resources of Ghana and Africa.

Through research, environmental monitoring, sustainable resource management, community engagement, and climate action, we work to safeguard biodiversity, strengthen rural communities, and promote a greener and more sustainable future for present and future generations.

Our purpose

A lasting commitment to Africa’s natural heritage.

We believe a greener future depends on protecting ecosystems and working together with the people who live alongside them.

Our Vision

To become a leading force in forest conservation and natural resource protection in Africa, where forests, biodiversity, and ecosystems are sustainably protected and preserved for present and future generations.

Our Mission

To protect, monitor, and preserve Ghana’s and Africa’s forests and natural resources through environmental surveillance, conservation advocacy, community engagement, research, and sustainable resource management, while promoting collective responsibility for a greener and more resilient future.

Our work

Four ways we work for a healthier future.

Our work connects sound environmental knowledge with practical action and local participation.

01

Research and Monitoring

Conduct environmental research, forest monitoring, and assessments to understand ecosystem health, identify threats, and support evidence-based conservation decisions.

02

Natural Resource Management

Promote sustainable use, protection, and management of forests, biodiversity, water, and other natural resources for present and future generations.

03

Rural Development

Work with rural communities and local stakeholders to promote sustainable livelihoods, environmental awareness, participation, and alternatives that ease pressure on forests.

04

Climate and Sustainability

Support climate mitigation and adaptation, ecosystem restoration, reforestation, and other activities that contribute to a greener, more climate-resilient Africa.
